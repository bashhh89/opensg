Scope of Work: Marketing Platform Migration & Content Strategy

Client: Aura Wellness Co.

Overview

Social Garden is pleased to present this bespoke Scope of Work (SOW) for Aura Wellness Co. This document outlines a comprehensive project to migrate your marketing operations from Marketo to HubSpot Enterprise, coupled with a strategic initiative to develop a new "Mindful Living" content pillar. Our approach ensures a seamless technical transition while simultaneously building a foundation for scalable, organic growth. The total investment targets your specified budget of approximately $80,000 AUD, and includes a 15% 'First Project' discount to signify our commitment to a strong, lasting partnership.

Project Outcomes

  • A fully operational HubSpot Enterprise instance, with all critical data, workflows, and assets successfully migrated from Marketo, enabling a unified and efficient marketing operation.
  • An empowered demand generation team, fully trained and equipped with optimized workflows and reporting dashboards to maximize HubSpot's capabilities.
  • A complete, actionable content strategy for the "Mindful Living" pillar, including a foundational SEO keyword framework and detailed plans for the first three long-form articles.
  • A scalable foundation for future marketing initiatives, with improved data integrity, streamlined automation, and enhanced reporting for data-driven decision-making.

Project Phases & Deliverables

This engagement is structured into two primary phases to ensure clarity and focus. Each deliverable is broken down into granular sub-tasks, reflecting our commitment to a transparent and strategic execution process.

Phase 1: Marketo to HubSpot Enterprise Migration (Est. 8-12 Weeks)

  • Discovery & Migration Blueprint:
    • Conduct a full audit of the existing Marketo instance to identify all assets, workflows, and data structures for migration.
    • Develop a detailed migration plan, including technical mapping, risk mitigation strategy, and a definitive timeline.
  • Technical Configuration & Data Migration:
    • Provision and configure the HubSpot Enterprise portal with user roles, permissions, and settings.
    • Execute a phased data migration, including a multi-stage data cleanse and validation protocol to ensure data integrity.
  • Workflow & Asset Reconstruction:
    • Rebuild and optimize core lead nurturing, scoring, and internal notification workflows in HubSpot.
    • Recreate essential assets such as email templates, landing pages, and forms to be on-brand and fully functional.
  • Team Enablement & Training:
    • Deliver comprehensive, hands-on training sessions for the demand generation team focused on practical, day-to-day use.
    • Create a custom 'HubSpot Playbook' with best practices and process documentation for ongoing reference.

Phase 2: "Mindful Living" Content Strategy Foundation (Est. 4-6 Weeks)

  • SEO Keyword & Topic Opportunity Analysis:
    • Conduct in-depth keyword research to identify high-value, low-competition keywords for the "Mindful Living" niche.
    • Perform a competitive content gap analysis to pinpoint strategic opportunities for thought leadership.
  • Foundational Content Planning:
    • Develop detailed content briefs for the first three long-form pillar articles, including outlines, target keywords, and internal linking strategy.
    • Create a content calendar for the first quarter, outlining publication dates and promotional channels.
