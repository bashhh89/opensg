### Sam's Ultimate Success Checklist

---

This checklist captures the complete success criteria for the AI SOW Generator, based on a deep analysis of the conversation. If the tool can pass every single one of these tests, it will be a definitive success.

### **Category 1: The "It Just Works" Metric - Speed & Usability**

This is about eliminating the core frustration: the tool feeling like a cumbersome, unreliable project instead of a solution.

*   **[✅] The "One-Shot" Generation Test:**
    *   Can he input a standard client brief and get a high-quality, 90% complete SOW on the very first try, with no errors, no missing hours, and no zeroed-out costs? The goal is to eliminate the "it didn't work" feedback loop entirely.

*   **[✅] The "Magic Wand" Revision Test:**
    *   When he gives a follow-up command like, "This is great, but make the total $45k AUD by reducing the specialist hours," does the tool execute the change perfectly across the entire document without him needing to specify every single line item? It should feel like a magic wand, not a negotiation.

*   **[✅] The "Zero Amnesia" Test:**
    *   Does the tool remember **every single rule, every single time**? Does it always use AUD? Is Account Management always last? Is the font always correct? It must be 100% consistent, requiring zero re-briefing on the core principles.

---

### **Category 2: The "This is Actually Smart" Metric - Content & Pricing Intelligence**

This is about the quality of the AI's output, moving beyond a simple template-filler to a strategic partner.

*   **[✅] The "Bespoke Strategist" Test:**
    *   Does the generated content read like a senior marketing strategist wrote it? Does it propose specific, insightful deliverables (e.g., "Phase 2: Lead Scoring Model Implementation & MQL Threshold Definition") instead of generic ones ("Phase 2: Setup")? This is the ultimate test of the "bespoke" requirement.

*   **[✅] The "Granularity & Role Logic" Test:**
    *   Does the pricing table correctly assign hours to specific, granular production roles instead of overloading expensive senior roles? Does it **always** include and price for the mandatory `Head Of`, `Project Coordination`, and `Account Management` layers?

*   **[✅] The "Numbers are Client-Ready" Test:**
    *   Are the numbers not just correct, but *smart*? Does the final total land on a clean, rounded number? If a discount is applied, is the math transparent and flawless? The pricing summary must be 100% ready to be sent to a client without manual calculator checks.

---

### **Category 3: The "Wow, This Looks Amazing" Metric - Professional Output**

This is about the final deliverable and whether it meets his standard for a client-facing document.

*   **[✅] The "Pixel-Perfect PDF" Test:**
    *   When he exports the final document, is the PDF absolutely flawless? Is the layout clean, with no messy formatting, weird line breaks, or awkward spacing? It must look professionally designed.

*   **[✅] The "Brand Identity" Test:**
    *   Does the final document perfectly reflect the Social Garden brand? Is the logo crisp? Is the **Plus Jakarta Sans** font used correctly? Are the **dark green** brand colors used for headings?

*   **[✅] The "Better Than Manual" Test:**
    *   Does the final SOW look as good as, or *better than*, the `Western Sydney University` example he spent two hours creating manually? The automated version should be the new gold standard.

---

### **Category 4: The "This Changes My Job" Metric - The True Business Value**

This is the ultimate bottom line, the real measure of success that goes beyond the tool itself.

*   **[✅] The "2 Hours to 10 Minutes" Test:**
    *   Has the tool reduced the time it takes to create a complex, high-quality SOW from a 2-hour manual ordeal to a 10-minute process of briefing, reviewing, and exporting?

*   **[✅] The "Confidence to Ship" Metric:**
    *   Does he **trust** the tool enough to use it for the "large number of new scope requests" he mentioned? Is he confident enough in the output to send it to a real client with only a brief review? This is the final, most important test.
